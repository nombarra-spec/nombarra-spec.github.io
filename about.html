About us

Nombara is built and operated by Naaz Global Corporation (Pvt) Ltd.

The company

Naaz Global Corporation (Pvt) Ltd is a company registered in Sri Lanka and based in Akurana, Kandy. It builds and operates Nombara.

Why Nombara exists

Visiting a doctor at a local dispensary in Sri Lanka too often means arriving early and waiting for hours. Nombara lets patients find a verified doctor, book a numbered token in the Nombara app, and follow the live queue from home — so they arrive when it’s nearly their turn. For clinics, it means a calmer waiting room and a queue that stays in order.

Nombara is built for local clinics, doctor by doctor. Every practitioner is checked against the right professional register — the Sri Lanka Medical Council, its dental register, or the Ayurvedic Medical Council — before patients can book them.
