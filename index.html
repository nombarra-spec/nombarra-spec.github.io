Find a doctor near you

How it works

  1. Find a practitioner. Browse verified doctors, dentists and Ayurveda practitioners — sorted by how near they are to you.
  2. Take a token. Pick a session and get a numbered token, from the app or from your browser.
  3. Arrive on time. The live “now serving” number and a near-turn alert tell you when to leave.

Nombara is a booking and queue service, not a medical provider. It does not give medical advice, diagnoses, triage or prescription handling — that stays entirely between you and the doctor.

No payments in the app

Nombara never takes a card payment and has no in-app purchases. The booking fee and the doctor’s fee are both paid in cash at the dispensary counter.

Every practitioner is verified

A practitioner appears on this site only after Nombara has checked their registration against the right professional register — the Sri Lanka Medical Council, its dental register, or the Ayurvedic Medical Council. Photos are published only with the practitioner’s consent.
